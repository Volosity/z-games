THE LANTERN BELOW -- how to play
Move with compass directions (NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST, UP, DOWN). LOOK and EXAMINE tell you what is
here. TAKE and DROP handle objects; PULL works levers; READ works tablets.
Combat is deliberate. Enemies announce (telegraph) their heavy blows one round before they land. In
combat:
  STRIKE (3 stamina, steady damage)
  HEAVY STRIKE (6 stamina, heavy damage)
  GUARD (blocks the next blow whole, restores stamina)
  EVADE (slips the next blow whole)
After a heavy blow, an enemy is EXPOSED for one round: strikes against it deal bonus damage -- the
punish window. Overspend stamina and you stagger, losing the turn.
REST at a lantern heals you, resets the dark's pieces, and lets you STRENGTHEN for glimmer. Death
drops all your glimmer where you fell; return and TAKE GLIMMER to recover it -- die again first, and
it is gone forever.
STATUS always reads your HP, Stamina, Glimmer, and last lantern. REPEAT re-reads the state of the
fight (pacing aid).
